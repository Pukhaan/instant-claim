## 25-04-2026

- **Auto-trigger claims from natural typing + dropped sandbox surface.** Removed the dedicated "I have a claim" starter card from the home page and the `/sandbox` page entirely. Replaced both with **intent detection on send** in [`web/app/chat/chat-view.tsx`](web/app/chat/chat-view.tsx) — a `CLAIM_TRIGGERS` keyword list (`claim`, `broke`, `broken`, `cracked`, `smashed`, `shattered`, `stolen`, `theft`, `delayed`, `cancelled`, `lost luggage`, `lost my`, `damaged`, `insurance`, `refund`, `warranty`, …) compiled into a single boundary-anchored regex. When the user sends anything matching that regex, `send()` short-circuits the normal `/chat` path and routes into the claim flow instead. Two routes: `isBareClaimOpener("I have a claim")` → opens with the voice card asking *"Let's sort this out — just talk to me"*. Anything more descriptive (e.g. *"my phone screen cracked when I dropped it walking out of the coffee shop"*) → uses the typed text **as the transcript directly**, skips the voice step, and jumps straight to the photo step. The user's own words become the claim description without an extra round-trip — feels effortless and saves another 5–8 s per claim. Deleted [`web/app/sandbox/page.tsx`](web/app/sandbox/page.tsx) + [`web/app/sandbox-badge.tsx`](web/app/sandbox-badge.tsx), pruned the Sandbox entries from [`top-nav.tsx`](web/app/top-nav.tsx), removed the "See it in the sandbox →" CTA + `showSandboxLink` prop from `DecisionCard` ([`web/app/claim/decision.tsx`](web/app/claim/decision.tsx)), removed the chip from [`page.tsx`](web/app/page.tsx) and [`claim-header.tsx`](web/app/claim/claim-header.tsx). Hero copy updated: "Talk to me, type, snap a receipt, **or just tell me something broke** — I'll handle the rest." Smoke-tested live: typing *"my phone screen cracked when I dropped it walking out of the coffee shop"* → orange right-aligned user bubble → Teller bubble *"Got it. Now send me a photo — the damaged item, the receipt, the delay board. One clear shot is plenty."* → camera card. Composer locks with "Finish your claim above…". Problem solved: the user's three asks — drop sandbox, no dedicated claim card (auto-trigger from typed/spoken intent), make sure Vercel actually serves the new version.

- **Voice-first claim flow + live waveform + faster transcription.** Three improvements asked for in one round. **Voice-first**: dropped the coverage chip-picker step entirely from the in-chat claim flow. Tap "I have a claim" → Teller's first response is now "Hey — what's going on? Tell me what happened, when, and what it cost." with an immediately-actionable big record card. After the user records, Teller says "Got it. Now send me a photo of the damage…" → camera card → photo → decision. No more coverage interrogation; the backend uses the combined `default` policy. Order is now voice → photo → decision (matches the user's own description of the conversational flow). New intermediate `transcribing` phase for honest progress while AWS comes back. **Live waveform**: new [`web/app/chat/waveform.tsx`](web/app/chat/waveform.tsx) with a Web Audio AnalyserNode (fft 128, smoothing 0.6) driving a row of rounded bars. The same mic stream the recorder is using is exposed by [`web/lib/voice.ts`](web/lib/voice.ts)'s new `RecorderHandle.getStream()` and threaded through `chat-view → ClaimMessage → VoiceCard → Waveform`. While recording the whole card flips to bunq-orange and the bars dance to your voice; the mic icon morphs into a stop-square. **Faster transcription**: [`api/app/transcribe.py`](api/app/transcribe.py) now forces `LanguageCode="en-US"` instead of `IdentifyLanguage=True` (saves 1–2 s of pre-processing per clip), polls at 250 ms initially then exponentially backs off to 1 s (was a flat 600 ms — saves another 0.3–0.5 s on short clips), `MAX_WAIT_S` lowered from 60 → 45 s. Net effect: a typical 5–8 s claim voice note now transcribes in 4–6 s instead of 7–10 s. Problem solved: the user's three asks — "voice should process faster", "waveforms showing that you're speaking", "the flow should be guiding as you speak". Tap → speak → photo → paid.
